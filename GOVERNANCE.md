# Governance

PandaPayLens is a founder-led, source-available project maintained by [Ratomir Jovanovic](https://github.com/ratomirjovanovic).

- **Decisions** are made by the maintainer after public discussion in issues or GitHub Discussions.
- **Code review** is public. Every change goes through a pull request with passing CI.
- **Releases** are approved by the maintainer and documented in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).
- **Maintainer access** is granted gradually to contributors with a record of trusted contributions.
- **Security issues** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Exceptions are documented without exposing vulnerabilities before a fix.
